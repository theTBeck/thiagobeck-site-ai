THIAGO BECK
AI Creative Director & Director of Photography

I have worked in film since 2003, across productions in Europe and South America. On the camera teams of features such as Mission: Impossible III, Quantum of Solace, Paolo Sorrentino’s La grande bellezza and Il divo, and Michele Placido’s Romanzo criminale, I developed an eye for light, movement and story. Today, that experience brings cinematography, VFX, 3D and AI together in films and brand campaigns.

Cinema studies at the Accademia Internazionale di Cinema in Cinecittà, Rome, and postgraduate studies in Arts at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Roma.
